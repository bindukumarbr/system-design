# Fast-Track: Module 16 - Compliance & Protection
**Core Concept:** WAF, RBAC vs ABAC, SOC2/GDPR, SBOM (Supply Chain).
**Case Study:** Price Tracker
- **Key Insight:** Hybrid RBAC/ABAC models for granular partner tiers. Data residency (EU data stays in EU).
- **Takeaway:** Add a WAF at the edge to block generic L7 attacks, and use ABAC for fine-grained resource permissions.

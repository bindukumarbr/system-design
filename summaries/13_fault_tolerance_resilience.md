# Fast-Track: Module 13 - Fault Tolerance & Resilience
**Core Concept:** RTO/RPO, Chaos Engineering, Bulkheads, Redundancy.
**Case Study:** Online Auction
- **Key Insight:** Single-writer-per-auction partition ensures linearizability without heavy distributed consensus. Idempotency keys are crucial for bids.
- **Takeaway:** RPO (Recovery Point Objective) and RTO (Recovery Time Objective) drive disaster recovery architecture.
